## 📌 Directory Purpose

The `insights/` directory serves as the centralized repository for empirical data outputs, calculated segment metrics, customer-level RFM scoring tables, and analytical summaries generated by the **E-Commerce RFM Customer Segmentation Engine**.

This documentation translates raw CSV data files into high-impact business insights, quantifying revenue concentration, customer lifetime value (CLV), churn risk exposure, and segment-level financial benchmarks across **94,989 unique customers** and **15,737,667.52 BRL (~15.74M BRL)** in total gross sales from the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset**.

## 🔍 In-Depth Empirical Data Insights

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        KEY EMPIRICAL INSIGHT HIGHLIGHTS                           |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. Revenue Concentration : Top 2 Segments (Loyal & Champions) generate 58.10%   |
|     (R$ 9.14M) of total platform sales.                                           |
|  2. Champion Powerhouse   : Champions achieve R$ 306.21 ARPU (2.53x Loyal ARPU). |
|  3. Churn Exposure Risk   : R$ 427.93K trapped in At-Risk cohort (ARPU R$ 225.23). |
|  4. Unrecovered Equity    : R$ 2.07M historical sales locked in 12,593 Lost users. |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1. High Revenue Concentration Among Top Behavioral Cohorts
- **Loyal** customers form the single largest user base (**41,740 users**, 43.94% of total base) and generate the largest share of cumulative revenue (**R$ 5.05M**, 32.12% of total sales).
- **Champions** represent a compact cohort of **13,354 users** (14.06% of total base) yet generate **R$ 4.09M** (25.98% of total revenue).
- Together, **Loyal** and **Champion** buyers generate **58.10% (R$ 9.14M)** of total enterprise revenue from 58.00% of the customer base.

### 2. Exceptional Financial Power of Champion Users
- **Champions** achieve an Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) of **R$ 306.21**, which is **84.8% higher** than the platform average (R$ 165.68) and **2.53x higher** than Loyal customers (R$ 121.10).
- This demonstrates that Champions either purchase premium, high-ticket items (electronics, furniture) or place multi-item order bundles, making them the most profitable segment per customer profile.

### 3. Immediate Churn Revenue Exposure (At-Risk Cohort)
- **1,900 high-value customers** are classified as **At Risk**, representing **R$ 427,933.15** in gross sales exposure.
- Crucially, the At-Risk cohort exhibits an ARPU of **R$ 225.23**—the **second highest ARPU** across all 5 segments (surpassing Lost, Loyal, and Others). These are premium spenders who are slipping into dormancy due to lack of timely retention engagement.

### 4. Trapped Revenue Equity in Lost Customers
- **12,593 users** belong to the **Lost** cohort, representing **R$ 2,073,734.79** in historical sales (13.18% of platform sales).
- These users have a solid historical ARPU of **R$ 164.67** but have gone cold. Re-activating just 5% of this group unlocks **R$ 103K+** in immediate re-engaged revenue.
