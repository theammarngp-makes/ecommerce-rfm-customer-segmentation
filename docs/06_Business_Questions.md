# 📄 Business Questions
## E-Commerce RFM Customer Segmentation

---

## ❓ Analytical & Strategic Questions Addressed

To transform raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence, this analysis systematically answers core strategic questions across five critical analytical dimensions:

---

### 1. Recency Dimensions (Customer Inactivity & Decay)
- **BQ-1.1:** What is the elapsed time distribution since the last purchase across the customer base?
- **BQ-1.2:** How many days of inactivity signify that a customer has entered the "At Risk" phase vs. total "Lost" churn?
- **BQ-1.3:** What proportion of gross revenue is generated by recently active customers (R-Score >= 4) versus dormant accounts?

### 2. Frequency Dimensions (Purchase Habituation & Repeat Rate)
- **BQ-2.1:** What percentage of customers are single-time buyers versus multi-transaction buyers?
- **BQ-2.2:** How does order frequency impact overall customer lifetime monetary contribution?
- **BQ-2.3:** Why does standard quintile binning (`NTILE(5)`) fail on raw order frequency in the Olist dataset, and how should SQL handle frequency skewness?

### 3. Monetary Dimensions (Revenue Concentration & ARPU)
- **BQ-3.1:** What is the total gross revenue contribution of each RFM customer segment?
- **BQ-3.2:** What is the Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) across Champions, Loyal, At-Risk, Lost, and Others?
- **BQ-3.3:** How heavily concentrated is Olist's revenue within the top 20% of customers (Pareto Principle check)?

### 4. Segment Breakdown & Risk Dimensions
- **BQ-4.1:** How many total unique customers belong to each of the 5 core RFM segments?
- **BQ-4.2:** What is the immediate financial revenue exposure trapped within the "At Risk" (R$ 427.93K) and "Lost" (R$ 2.07M) segments?
- **BQ-4.3:** Which customer segment represents the highest potential ROI for upselling and cross-selling campaigns?

### 5. CRM & Strategic Playbook Dimensions
- **BQ-5.1:** What specific marketing automation triggers should be configured for Champions vs. At-Risk buyers?
- **BQ-5.2:** How can CRM teams reallocate promotional budgets away from generic blasts toward high-LTV segment retention?
