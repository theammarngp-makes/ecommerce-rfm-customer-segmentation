## 🏬 E-Commerce Ecosystem & Platform Context

The e-commerce landscape in Brazil has experienced rapid structural growth, driven by increasing smartphone penetration, expanding digital payment infrastructure (Pix, boleto bancário, credit installment plans), and shifting consumer expectations toward online retail. 

This project utilizes transaction data from **Olist**, the largest e-commerce department store marketplace in Brazil. Olist functions as a marketplace integrator connecting small and medium-sized merchants (SMEs) to major Brazilian retail channels (such as Walmart, Carrefour, Casas Bahia, and Extra).

```
+-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+
|  SME Merchants    | ---> |  Olist Platform   | ---> | End Consumers     |
| (Product Supply)  |      | (Catalog & Ops)   |      | (94,989 Unique)   |
+-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+
```

### Marketplace Operating Dynamics:
1. **Multi-Seller Sourcing:** Customers purchase products spanning over 70 distinct categories (Electronics, Health & Beauty, Bed & Bath, Furniture, Toys, Sports) fulfillment-handled across distributed sellers.
2. **Customer Relationship Model:** Olist manages consumer-facing branding, payments, logistics notification, and customer support, making customer retention and lifecycle management a platform-level strategic priority.
3. **Transaction Architecture:** The platform processes multi-item orders with freight variations across 27 Brazilian states, creating diverse purchasing frequency patterns and monetary spend profiles.

## 📈 Macro-Economic & Strategic Drivers

| Strategic Driver | Market Reality | Business Impact on Olist |
|---|---|---|
| **Rising Acquisition Costs (CAC)** | Digital advertising CPMs increased significantly across Latin American channels. | Acquiring new single-time buyers yields diminishing returns; platform must maximize Lifetime Value (LTV). |
| **Logistics & Delivery Complexity** | Regional shipping times across Brazil vary from 2 to 45 days. | Delivery friction disproportionately impacts customer recency and repeat order probability. |
| **Payment Installment Culture** | Over 70% of Brazilian online purchases utilize credit card installments. | Monetary order value varies substantially based on payment flexibility. |
