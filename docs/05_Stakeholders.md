## E-Commerce RFM Customer Segmentation

---

## 👥 Executive & Operational Stakeholders

This analytical engagement supports key decision-makers across Olist's executive leadership, marketing operations, CRM teams, and business intelligence divisions.

| Stakeholder Role | Primary Responsibilities | Key Pain Points | Required Analytical Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)** | Overall marketing strategy, brand equity, customer acquisition & retention spend. | High customer churn; inability to justify retention campaign ROI to C-Suite. | Executive Summary, Revenue Contribution Breakdown, High-Level Recommendations. |
| **Head of CRM & Retention** | Customer lifecycle campaigns, email/SMS automation, loyalty program design. | Lack of dynamic customer segments; generic blast communications. | RFM Segment Mapping, At-Risk & Lost Cohort Files, Campaign Trigger Frameworks. |
| **Performance Marketing Lead** | Digital ad spend, retargeting campaigns, CAC optimization across ad platforms. | Wasted ad spend retargeting low-intent single-purchase customers. | Segment-Level ARPU Profiles, Recency Decay Curves, Target Audience Criteria. |
| **VP of Analytics & BI** | Data governance, reporting architecture, business intelligence infrastructure. | Non-standardized SQL queries, unmaintained analytics code, lack of documentation. | Optimized SQL Pipelines, Modular Python Scripts, Data Dictionary, ERD Schema. |
| **Category & Merchant Managers** | Vendor relations, product selection, category cross-selling initiatives. | Understanding which product categories attract Champions vs. single-order buyers. | Monetary Segment Breakdown, Cross-Category Opportunity Matrix. |
