# 📄 Executive Conclusion & Portfolio Synthesis
## E-Commerce RFM Customer Segmentation

---

## 📌 Executive Summary of Achievements

This project successfully transforms raw transactional records from the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset** into a production-grade, consulting-level **RFM Customer Analytics Engine**. By evaluating **94,989 unique customer entities** and **15,737,667.52 BRL** in cumulative gross sales, the analytical model unlocks granular behavioral insights into customer retention, churn risk exposure, and revenue concentration.
