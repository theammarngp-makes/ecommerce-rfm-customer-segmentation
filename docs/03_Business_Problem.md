# 📄 Business Problem Statement
## E-Commerce RFM Customer Segmentation

---

## 📌 Problem Overview

Despite generating **15,737,667.52 BRL** in cumulative gross sales from **94,989 unique buyers**, Olist faces a severe **customer retention and loyalty deficit**. Historical transaction data shows that the vast majority of consumers treat Olist as a single-use transactional marketplace rather than a repeat shopping destination.

Unsegmented marketing strategies and static customer engagement protocols have resulted in high customer turnover, missed upselling opportunities among high-value buyers, and unaddressed churn among previously active customers.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Challenges

### 1. High Churn & Silent Customer Loss
- **R$ 2.07M in Lost Customer Revenue:** Over **12,593 customers** (13.26% of the customer base) have churned completely (lowest recency rating). These users previously spent R$ 2,073,734.79 on the platform, representing a massive loss of historical customer equity.
- **R$ 427.93K Immediate Revenue at Risk:** An additional **1,900 high-value customers** (ARPU R$ 225.23) are categorized as **At Risk**, exhibiting extended periods of inactivity. Without targeted intervention, these high-monetary users will transition into total churn.

### 2. Unequal Customer Value & Misallocated Marketing Budget
- Treating all 94,989 customers with uniform, blast-style promotional campaigns results in severe marketing waste.
- **Champions** (13,354 users) spend **R$ 306.21 on average**, whereas **Loyal** users spend **R$ 121.10**. Broad discounts dilute margins on Champions who would purchase without aggressive price cuts, while failing to offer proper incentives to convert single-order buyers into loyal repeat shoppers.

### 3. Lack of Behavioral Visibility & CRM Personalization
- Without dynamic RFM segmentation, the marketing and growth teams cannot identify:
  - Which customers require VIP retention programs vs. reactivation campaigns.
  - Which buyers are at immediate risk of churn.
  - How customer recency correlates with purchase frequency and lifetime monetary value.
