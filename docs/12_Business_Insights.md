## 🔍 In-Depth Empirical Findings

### 1. Revenue Concentration Among Top Behavioral Cohorts
- **Loyal** customers form the largest customer volume (**41,740 users**, 43.94% of total base) and generate the largest share of revenue (**R$ 5,054,591.30**, 32.12% of overall gross sales).
- **Champions** represent a compact cohort of **13,354 users** (14.06% of total base) yet generate **R$ 4,089,088.19** (25.98% of total revenue). 
- Together, Loyal and Champion buyers generate **58.10% (R$ 9.14M)** of total enterprise revenue from 58.00% of the customer base.

### 2. Exceptional Financial Power of Champion Users
- **Champions** achieve an Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) of **R$ 306.21**, which is **84.8% higher** than the overall platform average (R$ 165.68) and **2.53x higher** than Loyal customers (R$ 121.10).
- This indicates that Champion buyers purchase higher-value goods (electronics, furniture) or multi-item order bundles, making them the most profitable segment per customer profile.

### 3. Immediate Churn Risk Exposure (At Risk Segment)
- **1,900 high-value customers** are categorized as **At Risk**, representing **R$ 427,933.15** in gross sales value.
- Crucially, the At-Risk cohort exhibits an ARPU of **R$ 225.23**—the second highest ARPU across all 5 segments (surpassing Lost, Loyal, and Others). These are premium spenders who are slipping into dormancy due to lack of retention engagement.

### 4. Trapped Revenue Equity in Lost Customers
- **12,593 users** are classified as **Lost**, accounting for **R$ 2,073,734.79** in historical sales (13.18% of platform revenue).
- These buyers have an ARPU of **R$ 164.67** but have been inactive for an extended period. Because they demonstrated substantial spending capacity, an automated win-back campaign targeting even 5% of this group unlocks **R$ 103K+** in immediate re-activated revenue.
