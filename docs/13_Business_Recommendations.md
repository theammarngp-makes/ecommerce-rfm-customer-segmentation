## 🎯 Strategic Action Matrix by Customer Segment

Based on empirical RFM segmentation analysis, Olist should deploy targeted, segment-specific CRM playbooks to maximize retention, protect revenue, and elevate customer lifetime value (CLV).

| Target Segment | Financial Impact | Primary Objective | Actionable Marketing & CRM Strategy | Campaign Channel & Automation Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🟡 **Champions** | R$ 4.09M (25.98% Rev) | VIP Retention & Margin Defense | Launch VIP Loyalty Tier ("Olist Select"), early access to sales, dedicated customer support, product preview access. | Exclusive Email, WhatsApp VIP Concierge, Push Notification. |
| 🟢 **Loyal** | R$ 5.05M (32.12% Rev) | Revenue Expansion & Cross-Sell | Deploy cross-selling recommendation engines based on past product categories; incentivize higher order values via free shipping thresholds. | Personalization Emails, In-App Banner Callouts. |
| 🟠 **At Risk** | R$ 427.93K (2.72% Rev) | Urgent Reactivation & Intervention | Trigger automated "We Miss You" reactivation sequences offering time-bound 15% discount codes within 14 days of inactivity threshold. | SMS Reminders, Retargeting Ads, Targeted Email. |
| 🔴 **Lost** | R$ 2.07M (13.18% Rev) | Win-Back Automation | Execute low-cost automated win-back email sequences highlighting new arrivals in previously purchased product categories. | Automated Lifecycle Email Triggers. |
| 🔵 **Others** | R$ 4.09M (26.00% Rev) | Nurturing & Activation | Run educational onboarding drips, customer review incentives, and second-purchase promotional offers to push users into Loyal tier. | Onboarding Email Drips, Retargeting Ad Campaigns. |
